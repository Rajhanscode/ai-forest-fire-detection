5. Real-World Applications and Future Potential of AI

AI in wildfire management is developing beyond simple fire detection. Modern systems can combine camera images, satellite observations, weather conditions, maps and historical fire data to give emergency managers a more complete picture of a developing wildfire. Companies and research organisations are also developing AI systems that can identify possible fires automatically and send alerts to human operators.

For example, Pano AI uses AI-powered cameras to continuously monitor areas for signs of wildfire, with human analysts involved in reviewing detections. Research is also being conducted into using AI to improve wildfire prediction and emergency response. ALERTCalifornia has reported collaborations exploring how AI can improve wildfire detection and response times. In the future, AI could therefore become part of a larger wildfire-management system combining satellites, cameras, sensors, weather forecasting, geographic information systems and human decision-making.

Sources: Pano AI, ALERTCalifornia, NASA FIRMS
