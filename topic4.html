4. Early Detection and Prevention: How Technology Can Reduce Fire Damage

One of the most important uses of AI and modern technology is reducing the time between fire ignition and detection. A fire detected while it is still small can potentially be contained before it spreads over a much larger area. Modern systems combine AI with surveillance cameras, satellites, weather information, geographical data and sensors to monitor large areas continuously.

ALERTCalifornia, for example, operates a large network of wildfire-monitoring cameras that can monitor remote areas during both daytime and nighttime. Satellites provide another important source of information. NASA's Fire Information for Resource Management System (FIRMS) uses satellite observations from instruments such as MODIS and VIIRS to detect active fires and thermal anomalies. Globally, FIRMS provides near-real-time fire information, with data generally becoming available within about three hours of satellite observation. These technologies do not replace firefighters; instead, they provide information that can help emergency teams respond more quickly.
