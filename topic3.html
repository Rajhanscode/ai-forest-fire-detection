3. Role of Artificial Intelligence in Forest-Fire Detection and Management

Artificial intelligence can help reduce wildfire damage mainly through early detection, risk analysis and decision support. AI systems can analyse images from surveillance cameras and satellites to identify smoke or unusual changes in the environment. They can also combine information about temperature, humidity, wind speed, vegetation and previous fires to identify areas at greater risk. AI can further assist in estimating how a fire might spread and where firefighting resources may be required.

This technology is already being used in real-world wildfire management. A notable example is ALERTCalifornia, a program associated with the University of California San Diego, which worked with CAL FIRE and DigitalPath to develop an AI system for wildfire detection. During its first season, the system was used across all 21 CAL FIRE dispatch centres and detected more than 1,200 fires. It detected fires before 911 calls more than 30% of the time, demonstrating how AI can provide an earlier warning to emergency responders.

Source: ALERTCalifornia, University of California San Diego, CAL FIRE
